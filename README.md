## ✨ Feature Matrix: The Four Pillars of Empowerment

Below is the living architecture of the trainer. Each module is independently toggleable, deeply configurable, and designed to work in harmony.

### ⚔️ Pillar One: Combat Mechanics — The Precision Edge
- **Hitbox Visualizer** : View precise collision frames in real-time, turning blind parries into calculated reads.
- **Stagger Meter Tuner** : Adjust the threshold before enemies flinch, allowing for more fluid combo chains.
- **Damage Feedback HUD** : A minimalist overlay that shows exact numerical feedback, color-coded by elemental affinity.
- **Auto-Riposte Windows** : Semi-automatic timing assist for perfect blocks—you still choose *when* to strike.

### 🏃 Pillar Two: Movement Fluidity — The Unshackled Stride
- **Momentum Retention** : Preserve sliding velocity after a dodge, enabling more natural parkour flow.
- **Climbing Grip Modifier** : Increase the grace period before hand-slip on wet surfaces.
- **Camera Damping Control** : Adjust the smoothing factor of the chase camera to your personal taste.
- **Fall Recovery Optimization** : Reduce the stun lock from high falls, making exploration less punishing.

### 🗺️ Pillar Three: Exploration Clarity — The Cartographer's Eye
- **Dynamic Waypoint Ghosting** : Show a translucent trail of your last 30 seconds of movement for backtracking.
- **Loot Range Indication** : A subtle ring around the player character, displaying the pickup radius for materials.
- **Weather Transmutation** : Visual-only alteration of weather effects (e.g., make rain lighter) to reduce eye strain *without* changing gameplay logic.
- **Ambient Audio Mixer** : Separate sliders for footsteps, wind, and dungeon echo for a personalized soundscape.

### 🧬 Pillar Four: Progression Curves — The Sculptor's Hand
- **Experience Flow Control** : Adjust the rate at which you gain skill points (e.g., ×0.5, ×1.5, ×2.0) to change the tempo of the grind.
- **Talent Tree Fog Remover** : Toggle the "undiscovered" blur on the skill map, laying bare all potential paths for planning.
- **Inventory Stack Sorter** : Auto-reorganize stacks based on weight-to-value ratio, clearing mental clutter.
- **Vendor Price Clarity** : A tooltip enhancement showing the *selling price trend* over the last 10 minutes of play.

## 🚀 Quick Start: Your First Forge Session

1. **Acquire the Artifact** — Place the trainer file in the same directory as the game executable. It needs no external runtime; it self-hosts.
2. **Run the Forge** — Launch the trainer before the game initially. A compact console window will appear, showing a "silent ignition" status.
3. **Bind Your Keys** — Use the `~` (tilde) key by default to open the **Master Control Panel**. Navigate via your keyboard (no mouse needed for speed).
4. **Toggle & Tune** — Every module can be activated with a simple hotkey (default `Numpad 0-9`), or you can dive into the deep settings menu for granular sliders.
